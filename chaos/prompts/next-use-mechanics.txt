SOURCE: src/chaos_next_use_runtime.c:chaos_next_use_on_action copies age as
monstermoves minus admission_move, counts from host runtime, and last accepted
state. These counts are host-bounded inputs, not callback invocation counters.

observation totals. Do not infer observed frequencies from them. Nonzero count
inputs in component fixtures are not evidence of nonzero production history.

SOURCE: chaos_next_use_on_action consumes the triggering slot on quiet or delay;

of the same family. Each admitted family supplies only one slot.
