Shared Gothic direction
Begin with intimate unease: an ordinary material detail, a precise sensory discrepancy, an uncertain interpretation. Let repetition or a small changed response earn dread. Gothic ambiguity can deepen toward Lovecraftian quest-scale mystery later, but this curio has only the present finite hooks; hint at uncertainty, not a promised quest or hidden world fact. Invent an original object and language. Literary influences guide craft, not a fixed story menu, catalogue, quotation or adaptation. Keep the prose spare enough for the display limits and mechanically truthful.
